# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users

Leaders who follow Pastor Olabanji Afolabi, split across two countries. Lagos
and the Nigerian diaspora on one side, Canada and the wider international
audience on the other. They arrive already trusting him, usually from a pulpit,
a stage or a book, and the job they are trying to get done is simple: work with
him. That means booking him to speak, joining one of three programmes, buying a
book, or reading what he writes.

The second user is Pastor Banji himself. He wants one place he controls, and he
does not want to chase anybody for access or payment.

### Brand Personality

**Weighty, warm, deliberate.**

He is a pastor and a leadership teacher, not a hustle coach. The voice is
measured and pastoral, never loud and never salesy. Authority comes from
restraint. Copy speaks plainly about carrying weight, character and building
people, because that is his actual subject matter.

The interface should feel like a well made hardback: considered, unhurried,
and worth keeping.

### Aesthetic Direction

Colours are taken from the brand mockups he supplied himself, sampled directly:

- Navy `#1B2237` (deep, desaturated, slightly cool)
- Gold `#C19C4D` (antique, muted, never bright or metallic)
- Ivory `#F4F3EF` for the light ground

Type is an editorial pairing: **Newsreader** for display, with a single word
italicised in headlines, and **Manrope** for body and interface.

Structure follows an editorial personal-brand direction: alternating dark and
light sections, large uppercase letterspaced serif section titles used as
dividers, portrait photography placed inside the content rather than only in
the hero, numbered cards for the three programmes, and a signature as a trust
mark.

**Use his own photographs.** This is a personal brand. Stock images of generic
speakers undermine the entire premise.

**Anti-references:** the generic modern-church template. Bright yellow on black,
a centred headline over a full-bleed stock photo with a heavy scrim, decorative
word chips that carry no information, and a page with no route to a booking or a
payment.

### Design Principles

1. **Every section must lead somewhere he gets paid.** Speaking to an enquiry,
   programmes to enrolment, books to checkout, writing to membership.
2. **Two countries, two rails, always visible.** Prices show in dollars and in
   naira, and the Nigerian route is never an afterthought.
3. **Authority through restraint.** Small body type, generous negative space,
   one gold accent used sparingly. Nothing glows.
4. **Structure carries information.** Numbers, labels and dividers encode the
   real sequence and the real categories, never decoration.
5. **Show the locked door.** The member area must make the paywall legible at a
   glance, because that is the part he cannot picture.
